# Privacy Policy – Glass Shift

**Developer:** MR and HS Devs
**Contact:** telkyexample@gmail.com
**Effective date:** November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game **Glass Shift** ("the App"), developed by MR and HS Devs ("we", "us"), handles information when you use it on Android.

## 1. Summary
- The App does not require an account or login, and we do not ask for your name, email, or other personal details.
- We do not run our own servers and we do not collect, store, or sell your personal data ourselves.
- The App shows ads provided by **Google AdMob**. Google may collect and process certain data as described below.
- All game progress and settings are stored **only locally on your device**.

## 2. Data stored on your device
The App saves the following data locally on your device only:
- level progress, stars, scores, and high scores;
- settings (music and sound volume, vibration, graphics quality, reduced effects, camera shake, language);
- a local counter used to decide when an ad may be shown.

This data never leaves your device through us. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show interstitial ads. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:
- device identifiers, including the Android Advertising ID;
- IP address and approximate location derived from it;
- device and app information (model, operating system version, language, app version);
- ad interaction data (ad impressions and clicks), and diagnostic and crash information related to ads.

Depending on your consent and your location, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. More information:
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads).

## 4. Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions)
The App uses Google's **User Messaging Platform (UMP)** to ask for your consent where required by law (for example under the GDPR). You can review or change your choices at any time in the App under **Settings → Privacy options** (shown when required in your region). If you do not consent, the App may show non-personalized or limited ads, or no ads. The game remains fully playable offline.

## 5. Sharing results
When you tap **Share**, the App creates an image of your result on your device and opens the Android share menu. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app and confirm the share yourself. We do not receive the shared content.

## 6. Permissions
The App uses the internet and network-state permissions only to load ads and consent forms. The App can be played without an internet connection. It may also use the vibration permission for optional haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.

## 7. Children
The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

## 8. Data security and retention
We do not collect personal data on our own servers. Data processed by Google is handled according to Google's policies. Local game data stays on your device until you delete it.

## 9. Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/EEA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not hold your personal data, requests about advertising data should be directed to Google. You can also contact us with any questions.

## 10. Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
